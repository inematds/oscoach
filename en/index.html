What Is an AI Operating System

One AI Operating System (AIOS) is, essentially, a command center for a specific domain of your life or business. It’s not a single product you install—it’s a handful of folders + an identity file (o CLAUDE.md), tool-agnostic (works with Claude Code, Codex, open code).

The author maintains several: a Freedom OS (residences and passports), a Tax OS (laws and audits), a Skool OS (ads in his voice), as well as Health OS and Marketing OS. Each domain becomes a custom OS. The golden rule: cultivate, don't install — a weekend gets you to 80%, but it only becomes solid with daily use and auditing.

The 6 layers—and the pie that isn’t divided equally

Every OS has the same six layers, but the "pie chart" changes shape with each domain: in a Tax OS, context weighs ~34%; in a Sales OS, tools and skills dominate. The order that works goes from foundation to action.

1 · Identity

The soul. Who the OS is, whom it serves, what it refuses. It lives in the CLAUDE.md.

2 · Substrate & Context

The behind-the-scenes layer. The raw and distilled knowledge the OS runs on. The largest layer.

3 · Rules & Hooks

The guardrails. A rule = a strong suggestion; a hook = deterministic (always/never).

4 · Skills

The verbs mastered. Repeatable tasks. Start manually, then capture them.

5 · Tools & Connections

The wires out. The question: skill, CLI, MCP, or API? Read-only CLIs for safety.

6 · Agents

Judgment-driven roles that orchestrate skills. The LAST layer — not the first.

OS Coach × OpenClaw: you build the foundation; it runs the agent

This course teaches you to structure the foundation: Identity, Context, Rules, Skills, Tools, and Agents. First you build the foundation; only then does an agent execute on top of it. That's where an execution platform like OpenClaw (the “Jarvis” that runs the agents): messages, automations, tools, and dashboards. The OS Coach prepares the foundation; OpenClaw runs the agent.
